## Nerd Stuff
I have way too many interests, but this is what you can expect to read about from me:

* ***Software Development:*** I have been known to develop full-stack applications when duty calls. I've got experience building apps across all kinds of languages and frameworks. My favorite languages are Python, TypeScript and PHP (Laravel, Angular and Flask are awesome. I think I'll build my next webapp with Django).

* ***Teaching:*** I'm a guy who knows a little bit about a lot of things. I've taught high school programming, competitive marching band, Bible studies, wrestling class, and probably other things too.

* ***Grappling:*** Currently, I am a BJJ purple belt and technically a Judo green belt, but I've been grappling for many years. I teach a wrestling class at my local gym and serve as an assistant coach at a local high school.

I'm a big fan of ministry and missions related audiobooks and podcasts (reading is just so boring to me, lol). I think two of the most influencial ministry/leadership books I've read so far include The Innovation Crisis by Ted Esler (love the Substack!) and The Making Of A Leader by J. Robert Clinton. I was never exactly a grinder, but there was a time when I travelled around the Midwest competeing in Modern with UR Murktide and CEDH with Atraxa.
